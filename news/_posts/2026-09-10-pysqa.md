title: pysqa published in Journal of Open Source Software 
---
The [Python Simple Queuing System Adaper](https://pysqa.readthedocs.io/) or [pysqa](https://pysqa.readthedocs.io/) for 
short was recently published in the [Journal of Open Source Software](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.10961). After the 
publication of [executorlib](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.07782) pysqa is the second component of pyiron being published
as standalone software package. [pysqa](https://pysqa.readthedocs.io/) provides a Python interface for a number of 
commonly used job schedulers in the high performance computing (HPC) context. Based on this, pysqa is the bases which is 
used by both [executorlib](https://executorlib.readthedocs.io) and [pyiron_base](https://pyiron-base.readthedocs.io)
