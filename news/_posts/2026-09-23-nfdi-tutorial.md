The [NFDI Matwerk](https://nfdi-matwerk.de) consortium aims to establish a digital infrastructure for Materials Science
in Germany. As part of this activity the consortium is organizing yearly summer school for students in the field of 
Materials Science. This years summer school took place at the [Ruhr University Bochum](https://www.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/).

The [2026 NFDI Matwerk summer school](https://www.eusmat.net/research/other-events/nfdi-matwerk-summer-school-2026/) 
covered a wide range of topics ranging from research data management based on electronic lab notebook to workflows. The 
pyiron tutorial primarily introduced [executorlib](https://executorlib.readthedocs.io/), 
[pyiron_workflow](https://pyiron-workflow.readthedocs.io/) and [pyironflow](https://github.com/pyiron/pyironflow) as 
well as how workflows can be shared between these three frameworks using the 
[Python Workflow Definition](https://pythonworkflow.github.io/).

The content of the tutorial is [available on Github](https://workshop.pyiron.org/NFDI-summerschool-2026/).
